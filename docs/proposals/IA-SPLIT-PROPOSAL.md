The last open piece of the reorg campaign (Phase 1.4 of the master brief):
declutter the System orrery group and home the orphan routes. Held for
sign-off because it needs a **new user-menu surface** and touches orrery
group **data** (never the animation — the bloom stays untouched).

## A. The problem, in numbers

- The **System** group carries 10 pills; half are personal utilities
  (Search, Pinned, Trash, Settings, Help), not "system" anything.
- **22 orphan routes** render fine but have no home in any orrery group:
  13 ERP back-office (`/admin/accounts` … `/admin/brain`), 6 internal/dev
  (`/me`, `/memory`, `/learning`, `/changelog`, `/design-system`,
  `/showcase`), plus `/roadmap`, `/brain/benchmarks`, `/brain/self-tuning`.

## B. Proposed moves (each independently approvable)

### B1. Personal utilities → a user menu (new component)
Build `src/components/layout/UserMenu.tsx` — avatar button in the Topbar,
opens a compact dropdown (same pattern as the compact mini-orbit you chose
in #263: anchored, never covers content):
**Search · Pinned · Settings · Trash · Help · /me · Logout.**
System group then shrinks to 5 honest pills: Mission Control, Workflows,
Integrations, Audit, Activity.
Change surface: one new component + Topbar slot + `groups.ts` data +
hand-sync `docs/design/orrery/index.html` GROUPS → `node
scripts/build/build-orrery.mjs`.

### B2. ERP back-office → one "The Core" hub pill
One new pill (suggested: in System, or Board — your call): **"النواة / The
Core"** → a hub page at `/admin` listing the 13 back-office consoles as
Heritage cards. No new routes; one hub page + one pill. (This is the Phase
27b item STATUS already tracks.)

### B3. Remaining orphans
| Route | Proposal |
|---|---|
| `/me` | into the user menu (B1) |
| `/changelog`, `/design-system`, `/showcase` | link them from `/help` (dev/reference shelf) — no pills |
| `/memory`, `/learning` | already exist as Brain pills (`/brain/memory`, `/brain/learning`) — the bare routes redirect there; no action |
| `/brain/benchmarks`, `/brain/self-tuning` | leave unlisted (reachable from `/brain`) or add to the Brain group — Brain group is at 13 pills, suggest leave |
| `/roadmap` | was deliberately dropped from the menu in #257-era trim — retire the route or leave unlisted; suggest leave |

## C. What will NOT change

- The orrery animation, bloom, and motion. Data arrays only.
- `public/orrery/` is regenerated by the build script, never hand-edited.
- No route is deleted; every move is additive or menu-level.

## D. Execution shape (once approved)

Two PRs, each green-gated + `next build` (layout chrome): `feat/user-menu`
(B1) then `feat/core-hub` (B2+B3). MiniOrrery/ConstellationRail inherit from
`groups.ts` automatically; the static hub gets its deliberate hand-sync step.

**To approve:** say which of B1/B2/B3 go ahead (and where The Core pill
lives). Anything rejected gets recorded in STATUS as deliberately unlisted.
